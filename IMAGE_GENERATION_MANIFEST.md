# FarmTree image generation manifest

Status: local pre-release audit, 2026-08-27. Generated images are editorial
illustrations, not evidence of a named FarmTree project, partner relationship,
person, outcome or certification. Human editorial approval remains required.

All new imagery follows the approved natural-documentary FarmTree language:
restrained forest greens and earth tones, credible field detail, natural light,
no embedded text, logos, flags, seals, watermarks or guaranteed outcomes.
Source PNGs live in `assets/media/source/news/`; responsive 1600 px and 768 px
WebP/AVIF derivatives live in
`wordpress/wp-content/themes/farmtree-native/assets/images/news/`. WordPress
Media Library uploads use the 1600 px WebP and WordPress-generated `srcset` and
dimensions. The 768 px derivative is the intentional mobile crop source.

## Editorial handling

- Generated source and derivative assets are project-bound to FarmTree.
- Preserve the documented alt intent when assets enter the Media Library.
- Do not use generated imagery as substantiation for claims or relationships.
- Do not generate or publish a named person's likeness without identity and
  publication-permission confirmation.
- Any future replacement must retain a center-safe crop and be verified at the
  nine target viewport widths.
